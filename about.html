It’s never too late to start!

Howdy! I’m thrilled to share a bit about myself and my journey with you. I am a Midwestern — born and raised in the Chicagoland area — who has found a love for traveling near and far. My career journey has taken me from classrooms to corporations, with each chapter teaching me valuable lessons. As a leader, I focus on coaching and mentoring my team to empower them to do their best work.

In my personal life, I have experience reacting to and initiating change. I came out as queer and non-binary in my mid-thirties. Many of my passions were only discovered in recent years — running, ice climbing, mountaineering — where I push myself to new distances/heights in order to better understand my capabilities. I feel it is never too late to discover new parts of yourself, and the most important thing you can do in your life is live with authenticity and passion.

As a Co-Active Coach, we will take active roles in helping you grow in whatever facet you’d like help on. I am your supporter, sounding board, and collaborator. Whether you are looking for coaching on leadership, career goals, personal changes, or more, we can work together to guide you down the path you choose!

My life goal is to be of service to others — to make a positive impact on people’s lives. Working together, I believe we can initiate positive change for you!

What I’m building

When something I wanted didn’t exist for a community I’m part of, I built it. Medaling with Friends is a social race schedule for runners. IceCycles is weather for ice climbers.

Same idea behind both as behind the coaching: people do better when they’re part of something that makes room for who they actually are.
