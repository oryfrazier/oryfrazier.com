Things I build

Both of these started the same way: I wanted something to exist for a community I’m part of, and it didn’t. So I built it.

Medaling with Friends

A social race schedule for runners

See what your friends are racing, share your own schedule, and find the people you know at the start line. Clubs and groups get their own space, and results come in automatically rather than being typed in by hand.

It’s private by default. Nothing is public unless you choose to make it public, and groups are invitation-only — because a race schedule tells people where you’ll be, and that shouldn’t be the default setting.

IceCycles

Weather for ice climbers

Ice climbing lives and dies on conditions. Too warm and the ice goes soft, too cold and it turns brittle, and the forecast you need isn’t the one on a general weather app.

IceCycles pulls the weather that actually matters for climbers deciding whether it’s worth the drive.

Why bother

The through-line is the same one that runs through my coaching: people do better when they’re part of something, and when the thing they’re part of makes room for who they actually are.

Endurance and outdoor sport aren’t always good at that. Building the tools is one way of pushing on it. Coaching is another.
